Security

Last updated: 9 June 2026

Security is a first-order feature for a product that watches your revenue. Here is how we protect it.

Data protection

  • TLS 1.2+ for all data in transit; AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Number plates are hashed with a per-tenant salt on the on-site device — readable plates never traverse the network.
  • Tenant data is logically isolated; access is scoped per account.

Access control

  • Role-based access in the dashboard (owner, operator, viewer).
  • SSO/SAML available on Group plans; enforced MFA for Laventra staff.
  • Least-privilege internal access, reviewed quarterly and logged.

Resilience

  • The on-site box buffers up to 14 days locally and syncs in order when connectivity returns — so an outage never costs you a count.
  • Encrypted, regularly tested backups with defined RPO/RTO targets.

Compliance

GDPR-aligned and CCPA-ready, with a signed DPA available. SOC 2 Type II is in progress; the current report and our latest penetration-test summary are available under NDA.

Reporting a vulnerability

We welcome responsible disclosure. Email security@laventra.io with details and we will acknowledge within two business days. Please do not access data that is not yours or degrade the service while testing.
