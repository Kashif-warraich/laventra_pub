Data Processing Addendum

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This page summarizes our DPA. A signable counterpart (incorporating the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum) is available on request from privacy@laventra.io and forms part of your agreement where you process personal data through Laventra.

Roles

For personal data processed through the service, you are the data controller and Laventra is the data processor, acting only on your documented instructions.

Subject matter & duration

Processing covers vehicle inference metadata, salted plate hashes, POS reconciliation records, and account data, for the duration of your subscription plus any agreed retention period.

Our obligations

  • Process personal data only on your instructions and for the purposes of the service.
  • Ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality.
  • Apply appropriate technical and organizational measures (see Security).
  • Engage sub-processors only under equivalent written terms, with prior notice and a right to object.
  • Assist you with data-subject requests, breach notification, and DPIAs.
  • Delete or return personal data at the end of the agreement, subject to legal retention.

International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA or UK, transfers are governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses and, where relevant, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

Sub-processors

A current list of sub-processors and their locations is available on request. We provide at least 30 days' notice before adding a new sub-processor.
